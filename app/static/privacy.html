Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

OmniPublish ("we", "our", or "us"), hosted at https://publisher.toomi.vn, provides social media publishing and workflow automation tools. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data of our users.

1. Information We Collect

When you use OmniPublish and connect your third-party accounts (such as TikTok, YouTube, and Meta/Facebook), we collect only the information necessary to fulfill your publishing requests:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and profile ID provided during registration or login.
  • Third-Party Platform Data: Authorized OAuth access tokens, profile identifiers, and channel/account names when you explicitly connect a social platform.
  • Content Data: Video files, titles, descriptions, and tags that you upload or provide for publishing.

2. How We Use TikTok and Third-Party Data

We use your authorized third-party platform permissions strictly for the following purposes:

  • Login & Authentication: Verifying your identity and maintaining your authenticated session.
  • Content Publishing: Uploading and publishing videos, captions, and tags to your designated TikTok account via official TikTok APIs upon your explicit command.
  • We never sell, rent, or transfer your social account tokens or personal data to any third parties or advertising networks.

3. Data Storage & Security

All sensitive authorization credentials, including OAuth tokens and secrets, are encrypted at rest using strong AES-256 encryption. Temporary media files are automatically removed after publishing or after the configured retention period.

4. Revocation & Data Deletion

You can disconnect your connected social accounts at any time through the "Social accounts" tab in the OmniPublish dashboard. You may also revoke access directly through your TikTok account security settings. Upon disconnection, stored authorization tokens are permanently deleted from our database.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: contact@toomi.vn.
